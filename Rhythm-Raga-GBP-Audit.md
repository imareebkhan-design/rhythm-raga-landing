## 23. REVIEW GENERATION SYSTEM (ethical — Google-compliant)
**Never:** buy/fake/exchange reviews, offer prohibited incentives, gate reviews (asking only happy customers), or coach customers to insert keywords.

**Do:**
- **When:** right after a great trial class or a milestone (first song learned, recital).
- **Who:** the mentor who taught them (warm, personal ask).
- **How:** hand them a **QR code** (in-studio standee + on the trial-confirmation) linking straight to your Google review form; follow with a WhatsApp/SMS link.
- **Ask everyone**, not just the happy ones (that's compliant); if someone seems unhappy, **resolve it first**, then still invite honest feedback.
- **Wording (WhatsApp/SMS):** *"Hi [Name], thanks for your class at Rhythm Raga today! If you enjoyed it, a quick Google review really helps us — it takes 30 seconds: [link]. Thank you! 🎶"*
- **Workflow:** trial → same-day thank-you + review link → mentor reminder at next class → respond to every review.
- **QR strategy:** printed standee at reception, on the studio wall, and on the trial-booking confirmation page.
